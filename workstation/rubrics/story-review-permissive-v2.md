# CrowdMovie Story Review Policy v2

The default verdict is **approve**. This is a broad creative-fiction platform,
not a family-friendly content filter. Reject only when the complete submission
clearly meets at least one hard refusal category below. If context is ambiguous,
satirical, critical, documentary, historical, or simply transgressive, approve.

## Hard refusal categories

1. **Sexualized minors or CSAM**: any sexual depiction, sexualized image, or
   exploitative sexual scenario involving a person presented as under 18.
2. **Primarily explicit pornography**: the work's main purpose is graphic sexual
   arousal. Romance, sexual themes, non-explicit intimacy, non-sexual nudity, or
   an isolated adult sexual reference is not enough to reject.
3. **Direct advocacy of crimes against humanity or real-world mass harm**: the
   submission sincerely promotes, recruits for, praises, or gives actionable
   encouragement for genocide, ethnic cleansing, mass extermination, systematic
   torture, slavery, or comparable large-scale attacks on civilians. Fictional
   depiction, a villain's dialogue, satire, criticism, historical portrayal, or
   an anti-war story is not advocacy and must pass.

## Content that must be allowed

- Fictional violence of any ordinary movie genre, including action, crime, war,
  horror, slasher, martial arts, weapons, blood, gore, death, and destruction.
- Dark comedy, profanity, political satire, offensive characters, villains,
  controversial ideas, and depictions of prejudice when they do not cross the
  direct-advocacy threshold above.
- Mature themes, frightening imagery, tragedy, substance use, and criminal acts.
- Rough art, unusual styles, imperfect captions, and minor image-caption
  differences. These are not safety refusals.

Do not invent additional platform rules. Do not reject merely because material
is violent, disturbing, politically controversial, rude, tasteless, or likely to
receive an adult rating. A refusal must name the exact hard category and the
specific submitted element that crosses it.
