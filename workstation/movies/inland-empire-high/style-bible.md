# South Park Cutout v1

Profiles: `south-park-cutout-v1` and
`touhou-inland-empire-high-six-v1`. Both are mandatory.

Directly use the recognizable visual language and performance form of the South
Park television show: crude, flat 2D construction-paper cutout adult satire.
Render the fixed Touhou cast through that language without copying any South
Park character, costume, logo, location, prop, or story element. Every principal
has a simple near-circular head, tiny compact geometric body, enormous side-by-
side white circular eyes with tiny black dot pupils, a small hinged paper mouth,
mitten-like hands, very few facial or clothing lines, and broad clean matte color
shapes bounded by thick, slightly irregular black outlines. Avoid anime anatomy,
anime facial construction, glossy highlights, detailed hair strands, gradients,
realistic lighting, depth of field, or elaborate surface texture.

Stage characters mostly front-on or in simple three-quarter profile. Movement is
deliberately stiff and jerky: whole paper pieces translate or rotate, mouths flap
between a few cutout shapes, eyes blink as simple layers, and poses snap instead
of deforming smoothly. Build Inland Empire public-school locations from simple,
shallow layered-paper shapes: stucco walls, windows, fences, bleachers, security
cameras, warehouses, desert plants, hazy sky, and dusty brown mountains. Keep
backgrounds readable but graphically simple, with minimal perspective and no
cinematic realism. Favor deadpan delivery, abrupt reactions, and sharp ensemble
political comedy.

This profile overrides conflicting submissions. Never dilute it into a generic
paper-craft look, storybook chibi, soft oval anime eyes, glossy plastic toys,
photorealism, live action, painterly rendering, detailed 3D CGI, or generic cel
anime. Do not introduce any South Park named character or trademarked in-world
asset. Do not depict real politicians, real party logos, hate symbols,
sexualized students, weapons, gore, captions, subtitles, watermarks, or
unapproved readable signs.

The 8191 gateway injects the fixed style and character anchors again. The
director must still author `h3_prompt_en` in this style and in the supplied
MiniMax official `h3-prompt-writing` T2VA structure: one continuous `[Shot 1]`,
then `integrated_multimodal_description`, `overall_soundscape`, and
`non_diegetic_music` in the official order. Include scene, camera, action,
English dialogue/audio, and ambience. Every AI Director creative source field
is English, including the episode outline, public shot pitch, scene summary,
continuity notes, dialogue, and audio instructions. Translated subtitle tracks
are the only non-English director output. Do not ask the workflow to draw
captions or subtitles.

For every speaking character, `h3_prompt_en` must explicitly state the fixed
English voice from `characters.json` immediately before the character's exact
line in a `<d>[English] ...</d>` block. Each `dialogue_en` line appears exactly
once and in order. No quoted speech, scene/round number, or numeric scheduling
range may appear outside those blocks; exact timing belongs in `dialogue_en` and
the workflow fields. The H3 node prompt must exactly equal `h3_prompt_en`. All
principals must audibly read as young girls or little girls. Never use adult
male, boy, mature-woman, deep, aged, or South Park character-imitation voices.
