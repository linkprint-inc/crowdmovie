# Who’s Next style bible

Profiles: whos-next-causal-cg-v1 and whos-next-famous-cast-v3.

Use clear, weighty high-detail game CG. Preserve canonical face, silhouette,
costume, palette, equipment and signature abilities. Establish each location
with fixed landmarks, concrete materials and a consistent light direction.
The selected episode sets its architecture and era; do not impose dark fantasy
on every venue or erase an established arena during a sampling reset.

Actions have anticipation, load, contact, reciprocal reaction and follow-through.
Broken cover stays broken; fragments, dropped equipment and exits matter to the
next beat. New fighters enter only after a visible departure; at most two are
active. Keep action and spatial depth readable, motion streaks local and camera
movement modest. Choose framing for a purpose; medium-wide is an option, not a
mandatory recipe. A cut may reveal a completed contact, reaction or new tactical
information while retaining the action axis.

Each 5–15 second generation changes the fight. A brief result hold is useful;
continuous attacks and cliffhangers are not compulsory. Scene count never
substitutes for an earned episode ending. The server chooses T2VA/I2VA independently
of story continuity and keeps the fixed FL2VA Acc 8 Step sampling baseline.

Speech uses exact English dialogue and stable original voice descriptions.
Impact sounds follow physical contact; ambient beds persist across continuous
scenes. Planned dialogue is not a transcript. Final subtitles use measured audio
and the same shared time axis for English, Chinese, Japanese and Spanish.
